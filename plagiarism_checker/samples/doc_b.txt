Machine learning, a branch of artificial intelligence, allows computational systems to learn from data and improve their performance without explicit programming. By training on sample datasets, these systems build predictive models. Deep learning extends this concept using multi-layered neural networks to process large volumes of information. This has led to significant advances across sectors such as medicine, banking, and autonomous vehicles. Research indicates that deep learning achieves an accuracy of 94.7% in image recognition benchmarks [1]. The cost of model training has also dropped by around 60% in five years [2].
