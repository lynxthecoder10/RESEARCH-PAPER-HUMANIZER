Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed. The process involves building mathematical models from sample data, known as training data, to make predictions or decisions. Deep learning, a further specialization, employs artificial neural networks with many layers to analyze large amounts of data. These technologies have transformed industries including healthcare, finance, and transportation. Studies have shown that deep learning models achieve an accuracy rate of 94.7% in image classification tasks [1]. Furthermore, the computational cost of training these models has been reduced by approximately 60% over the past five years [2].
